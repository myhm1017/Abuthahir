hearby♥

Miles apart, a heartbeat away.

A working preview of the Hearby home-screen widget, shown with example names Leo (London) and Mia (Toronto). You can rename them under each phone. Send something from one phone and watch it land on the other.

Things to try

  • The small widget just plays the painted scene. Watch the partner tease you: knocking on your screen, poking your cheek, squishing their face against the glass, playing hide and seek, or blowing bubbles.
  • Tap the widget to open it. The bigger view has the message and the buttons: 📷 photo, 🖊️ text, 💬 preset and 💗 felt it.
  • Send "mwah 😘", "sending a hug" or "good night 🌙". The animation changes with the meaning, and it's a little different every time.
  • Watch the other phone do the 2-beat heartbeat. On an Android phone, your device may actually vibrate.
  • Turn on Asleep for Mia, then send from Leo. It arrives quietly and the scene turns to night. Turn Asleep off to feel the heartbeat.
  • Send from both phones within 15 seconds for a rare in-sync hug.
  • Turn on Discreet mode to blur messages until you tap them.
  • Special days: open a widget and tap 📅. Birthdays, anniversaries and other moments are listed with a countdown. Add your own, and choose a celebration (confetti, balloons, fireworks, light show, drone show or everything). Mia's birthday is set for tomorrow, so Leo gets a private tease a few seconds after the page loads. Tap ⏰ to preview a day-before reminder or 🎉 to preview the celebration on both phones.
  • Scenery: every time someone looks at their screen (screen on, switching phones or coming back to the page), the widget moves to a new place and season: Santorini, the Swiss Alps, Mount Fuji, the Grand Canyon, a Banff trail, the Maldives, an Icelandic waterfall, Kyoto, Central Park, Dubai, Paris, the Taj Mahal, the Pyramids, the Golden Gate, Cappadocia and a Norwegian fjord, in spring, summer, autumn or winter. Pick a fixed place or season under each phone if you prefer.
  • Sleep together and date nights: sleep together (both screens turn into the same starry sky, with optional rain, ocean or soft noise), and plan date nights with when, where, food, an activity and a movie.
  • Albums and bucket list: make albums from photos and play them as a slideshow, on your widget, or send them to your partner's widget. Bucket list places show as little scenery pictures in your dream season.
  • The full app: tap the 💗 Hearby icon on either home screen (or the partner's name in the open widget). Four tabs: Home (a swipeable, auto-playing slideshow of your photos, quick sending, mood check-in, today's question, next special day and date night, this week's love stats, and the distance and time between you), Together (sleep together, date night planner, dua list), Memories (Memory Jar, albums, timeline, "open when" letters, bucket list) and Me (your character, secret gift ideas, privacy, settings).
  • Gentle reminders: every now and then (about once a minute in this preview, a few times a day in the real app), each phone gets a soft nudge to message or check on the other, or to make dua for each other and for you both, such as "Take a moment to make dua for Mia and for you both 🤲". Choose love and dua, love and prayer, love only, or off under each phone, or tap "Show a reminder now".
  • Meet-up countdown: open a widget and tap the ✈️ chip to set or change the date, add a note, choose "We don't know the date yet" (shows "soon"), or hide the countdown. Both phones update.
  • Teasing reminders: the day before, the app only asks something cheeky like "What day is tomorrow 😉?" and leaves them to work it out. On the day, a clear notification says what day it is, and if nobody has sent a wish about 15 seconds after the celebration (hours later in the real app), it nudges them: "Have you wished Mia yet? 👀 Just checking…".
  • Repeating days: special days can repeat every year, month, week or day, or happen just once. A monthly "Our monthversary" on the 9th is already in the list.
  • Customize the characters: under each phone, open "Customize … name and look" to rename them or change their style: hijab styles (classic, khimar, turban wrap, niqab), hair (buzz cut, quiff, man bun, wolf cut, braids, bun and more), 13 beard and moustache styles, tops, bottoms, dresses (abaya, gown, churidar, saree, burqa and more), shoes, skin tone, eye color, hair or hijab color, outfit, facial hair and glasses.
  • Slideshow: open the widget, tap 📷, pick a few photos (or "Use sample photos"), choose a transition and send. The other widget starts a slideshow. Tap 🎞️ to switch between the slideshow and the couple scene.
  • Pop-up text: tap 🖊️, choose Pop-up ✨ and send. It types out in huge letters over a blurred background and slides across like a news ticker.
  • Flying kiss or heart: tap 💋 or ❤️. It flies at the other person, grows to fill the widget and fades.
  • Pop-ups, kisses and hearts only play while the person is looking. Turn off Mia's screen (the side button or the "Screen on" switch), send one from Leo, then turn the screen back on.

This is a design preview. In the real Android app the heartbeat is a true vibration, messages are end-to-end encrypted, and the widget plays the scenes as short looping animations.
