hearby♥

Miles apart, a heartbeat away.

A working preview of the home-screen widget. Leo is in London and Mia is in Toronto. Send something from one phone and watch it land on the other.

Things to try

  • Send "mwah 😘", "sending a hug" or "good night 🌙". The animation changes with the meaning, and it's a little different every time.
  • Watch the other phone do the 2-beat heartbeat. On an Android phone, your device may actually vibrate.
  • Tap Felt it on the receiving phone so the sender knows their love landed.
  • Turn on Asleep for Mia, then send from Leo. It arrives quietly. Turn Asleep off to feel the heartbeat.
  • Send from both phones within 15 seconds for a rare in-sync moment.
  • Turn on Discreet mode to blur messages until you tap them.

This is a design preview. In the real Android app the heartbeat is a true vibration, messages are end-to-end encrypted, and widget animations run as short flipbook loops.
